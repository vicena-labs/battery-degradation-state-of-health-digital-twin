# R&D roadmap

Part of the [Vicena Research Twins collection](https://vicena.ai).

1. Calibrate ECM parameters from pulse and impedance experiments.
2. Add temperature-dependent OCV and hysteresis.
3. Link capacity fade and resistance growth to operating histories.
4. Add bootstrap or conformal prediction intervals by held-out cell.
5. Validate cycle-aging and RUL on independent cell campaigns.
6. Add pack topology, imbalance, cooling, estimator latency, and fault injection.
7. Define Level 2 acceptance gates before operational decision support.
